Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

JoeunLand collects minimal information required to operate the service via TikTok OAuth 2.0 authentication. When you connect your TikTok account, we access only the permissions you explicitly grant, such as basic profile information (display name and avatar) and video publishing permissions.

2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok account and display your connected profile status.
  • Upload and publish real estate video content to your TikTok account upon your explicit request.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Authentication tokens are stored securely and are never sold, rented, or shared with unauthorized third parties. We do not collect or store your TikTok password.

4. Data Deletion and Revocation

You can disconnect your TikTok account and revoke access permissions at any time via your TikTok app settings. Upon disconnection or request, associated access tokens are permanently deleted.
